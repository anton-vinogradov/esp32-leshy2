# Leshy2 — layout render spec

*Read this in: **English** · [Русский](layout-spec.ru.md)*

The fixed spec for (re)drawing the two-board clamshell layout render ([`layout-clamshell.en.svg`](img/layout-clamshell.en.svg) / [`.ru.svg`](img/layout-clamshell.ru.svg)). It is a **stage-5 artifact on par with the render itself**: the render must always be reproducible from this spec, and when the two disagree, **this spec wins and the render is regenerated**. Footprints are the real datasheet bodies of the parts chosen in the [tscircuit sheets](../hardware/tscircuit/).

The render is generated by [`img/leshy2_layout.py`](img/leshy2_layout.py) — the reproducible source, which self-checks the render against §8 before writing it.

> **These rules bind the physical PCB layout (stage 8), not only the render.** The real board must place every **interface, antenna, connector and control** exactly where this spec puts it — those positions cut the case and define the ergonomics. Internal **components** are free to sit anywhere **inside their assigned area** (§5a). The render is the contract the PCB implements.

## 1. Canvas & structure

- **Portrait, three rows.** Row 1 = main board (**outer front** | **inner**); Row 2 = C5 board (**inner** | **outer back**); Row 3 = side **cross-section** + antenna table + legend.
- **Bilingual** — one SVG for EN, one for RU, same geometry.
- **To scale** — both boards **~75 × 150 mm**, drawn at one fixed px/mm; every part is drawn at its real footprint (§6–§7).

## 2. The two boards

- **Board size: ~75 × 150 mm each; ~36 mm total clamshell thickness (11 mm gap + 18.6 mm battery + boards + display).**
- **Clamshell.** Inner faces face each other — components live in the gap, protected. Outer faces carry only the display + controls (front) and the battery (back), **plus the antenna SMA across the top of each**.
- **Mezzanine gap.** The standoff height is **≥ the tallest inner-face component**, so the two boards' inner parts clear each other: `h_A(x,y) + h_B(x,y) ≤ gap` everywhere. The **nRF24 modules are the tallest inner parts** — mounted **low** (soldered / short pins, DIV-style, **not** on tall 2×4 sockets) they stand only **~5–7 mm**, so the gap follows the **DIV-measured 11 mm gap** (a tall socket would add ~8 mm and blow the thickness budget — don't use one).
- **Mezzanine connector.** The board-to-board link is an **SMD mezzanine / stacking connector** on the two **inner** faces, spanning the gap. It lives **entirely inside the gap** and does **not** protrude onto the outer faces — it is **not** a through-hole part with pins poking out the front / back. Its footprint sits interior-lower and **centred**, clear of the display (main outer) and the battery (C5 outer) directly behind it. **The two halves must mate:** both land at the **same device X and Y** (X via 75 − part-x, and the **same** Y — Y is not mirrored) with a **mirrored pinout**, or they cannot connect across the fold (**check 7**).
- **Mounting.** Four **M2.5** corner holes, aligned on both boards; **every part and antenna is kept clear of them**.

## 2a. Assembly fold (physical, not the drawing mirror)

Distinct from the drawing mirror of §3. When the two boards close inner-face-to-inner-face (top stays up), the C5 board seats **X-mirrored** relative to the main board — a C5 part at part-x sits at **device-x = 75 − part-x** in the assembled device (a footprint **interval** `[x, x+w]` maps to `[75−(x+w), 75−x]`; **device-y = part-y** is unchanged). So a component on the C5's **right** side edge ends up on the device's **left** side edge, sharing that case slot (and the ~11 mm gap band) with whatever the main board puts on its own left edge. This is why edge connectors on the two boards can collide even though each passes its own per-face checks — **check 6** (§8) catches it, and it **binds the physical PCB layout**: on the real boards, place the two boards' side-edge exits so they do not fold onto the same slot. (Bottom-edge exits sit ~11 mm apart in depth on the ~36 mm-deep bottom face, so they may share an X position.)

## 3. Views & the mirror rule

- Four faces are drawn: main outer, main inner, C5 inner, C5 outer, plus the cross-section.
- **Inner faces are true back views — mirrored left↔right.** An inner face is the front flipped about the vertical axis: **top / bottom stay, left and right swap**. A part on the device's **left** edge is therefore drawn on the **right** of the inner view (and vice versa) — the two views describe the same physical board, not an X-ray *through* it.
- **Cross-section** is the thickness stack front→back (display · main board · gap · C5 board · battery), with the gap ≥ the tallest inner part (§2).

## 4. Interface vs component (colour)

- **Interface — blue frame.** Anything a human contacts: **screws an antenna, plugs a cable, presses, inserts a card, slides, speaks / listens, touches the screen.** The **battery holder** is an interface (you load the cells) — green fill + blue frame.
- **Component — grey frame.** Internal, never touched: MCUs, radios, ICs, decoders, `BT1`, the mezzanine.
- **Indicators** are their own marks, not framed boxes: TX-live LEDs = small amber dots, RGB status = an R/G/B pie.
- **Silkscreen labels — teal monospace.** An interface's label *is* the text printed on the PCB silkscreen next to it (`SPK`, `MIC`, `JACK`, `RST`, `BOOT`, `USB J1/J2`, `master`, `OK`, `nRF1`…, the antenna names). It is drawn teal + monospace so it reads as an on-board legend. Everything else — component names, the area description, cross-section notes — is plain grey/black and is **not** silkscreen. Keep silkscreen labels short but legible (fuller than cryptic: `nRF1`, `CC1101`, not `nR`, `CC`).

## 5. Placement rules

- **Outer front — the display + a *centred* D-pad + BACK / OPTIONS (flanking it, on one horizontal line with the D-pad) + the LED row.** The LED row is the **7 discrete-radio TX-live LEDs** — one per chain that has a hardware envelope detector: **3× nRF24, CC1101, SA868, LoRa, IR** (Wi-Fi and 5 GHz have no discrete detector — they live inside the ESP modules, so they get no TX LED). Spread across the full width so each carries a silkscreen label, with the **RGB status** at the end. Antennas across the top. Nothing else.
- **Outer back — the battery, ringed by the controls:** the 2× 18650 holder (centred) with the **encoder above it** and **F1 / F2 to its left, PTT + STOP to its right** — **face buttons like BACK/OPT** (pressed from the back face, not side-actuated), inset from the edge. The **C5 reads the encoder + these buttons locally and relays them to the S3 over the link** — which frees the crowded main inner and further relieves the S3. The encoder is a **scroll / side type**, not a top-shaft EC11 (its 15 mm shaft would break the 11 mm gap). Antennas across the top, on a **5-slot grid with one interior slot skipped**, so the **3 nRF antennas land at the two extremes + centre (maximally spread)** and the 5 GHz takes the remaining interior slot.
- **IR = two parts** — an **IR TX emitter + an IR RX receiver**, both on the C5 board's **right** side edge, firing out between the boards (DIV-style). **Speaker (round mylar)** on the C5 inner face, lower and **horizontally centred** — a **round** grille firing forward. The **mic (MEMS)** lives on the **main inner bottom edge**, **horizontally centred**, firing **down** (moved off the C5 side so the fold stays clean — see the connectors rule below).
- **All connectors / switches / service buttons go on the INNER faces**, each **at the board edge it exits** (a part mid-board can't reach a case slot), reached through slots in the case edge. **Side-edge exits on the two boards must not fold onto the same device-edge slot** (§2a, check 6). The clean way is to keep each device side edge **single-board**: the C5's IR owns the device-**left** edge, so the main's 3.5 mm jack sits on the main's **right** side edge (above the 2× Grove), and the mic moves off the C5 side to the main's bottom edge — no two boards share a side.
- **Direction arrow on every external port / side part** — a small red arrow **pointing out from the part into the margin, never onto a label** (checked). Bottom-edge → down; side-edge → out to that side. Only the **round speaker** is a forward-firing grille (no arrow, exempt from the edge rule); the **mic** exits the bottom edge (down arrow) and is a normal edge port.

## 5a. Floorplan zones

Interfaces are placed **exactly** (they cut the case). Internal components are **not placed individually in this artifact** — each inner face carries a single **component area** (dashed outline, labelled *"placed in PCB layout, stage 8"*), and the parts are positioned inside it during stage 8. Zones never overlap; the checks enforce it. Edges below are named in each board's **part-frame**; the C5 folds into the device frame (§7 / §2a), so a C5 edge lands on the opposite device edge.

- **Main outer:** *antenna row* (top) · *display* (upper two-thirds) · *control strip* (bottom third: LED row, centred D-pad, BACK/OK/OPT on one line).
- **Main inner:** *component area* (interior upper — S3, SA868, LoRa, CC1101, GPS, Si4732 + audio, buses, LCD driver) · *right edge* (jack **above** the 2× Grove) · *mezzanine* (interior lower, centred) · *service row* (bottom: **mic centred**, with microSD to its left and RESET / BOOT to its right). The left edge is left free (the C5's IR folds onto it).
- **C5 inner:** *component area* (interior upper — C5, 3× nRF24, charger + power, IR driver, 74HC139, PCA9555, BT1) · *audio* (lower: **centred** round speaker) · *right edge* (IR TX + IR RX) · *mezzanine* (centred) · *service row* (bottom: USB ×2, master, RESET, BOOT).
- **C5 outer:** *antenna row* (top — 5-slot grid, 1 slot skipped) · *battery zone* (centre: the 2× 18650 holder) ringed by controls — *encoder* (above), *F1/F2* (left), *PTT/STOP* (right).

## 6. Proportions — real footprints

Every box ≈ its **real PCB footprint** (§7), to the one common scale — **a connector is never drawn larger than a module**. Shapes:

| Shape | Drawn as | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| `module` | rounded rectangle (RF-shield hint) | WROOM, nRF24, SA868, LoRa, GPS |
| `IC` | small rectangle | CC1101, Si4732, PCA9555, chargers, LDOs |
| `connector-rect` | small rectangle at an edge | USB-C, microSD, jack, Grove, slide |
| `connector-round` | circle (hex hint) | SMA |
| `cylinder` | rounded capsule | 18650 cell |
| `holder` | large rounded rectangle | 2× 18650 holder |
| `button-square` (`button`) | small square | D-pad, encoder, tact buttons |
| `led` | small dot / pie | TX-live, RGB |
| `display` | large rectangle (active-area inset) | ST7796 |
| `port` | tiny rectangle at an edge | MEMS mic |
| `patch` | square (ceramic-patch hint) | GPS |
| `grille` | circle with concentric rings | round speaker |
| `emitter` / `receiver` | small rounded rectangle | IR TX / RX |
| `mez` | rounded rectangle, interior | mezzanine connector |
| `hole` | ring | M2.5 mounting hole |

*`IC grp` = a labelled group of small ICs drawn as one box. Parts smaller than **~5 mm** (mic, IR TX/RX, the ~3 mm power ICs, the encoder/master) are drawn **slightly enlarged** so their silkscreen label stays legible — the **footprint mm in §7 is authoritative**, not the drawn size.*

## 7. Component placement table

Footprints are the **real datasheet body / footprint W × H in mm** (length is the larger dimension). `main` = S3 main board, `C5` = C5 board; `F` = outer face, `I` = inner face.

**Frame of reference.** Positions are given in each board's own **part-frame** (that board seen with its components facing up, x = 0 left … 75 right, y = 0 top). The **main** board's part-frame **is** the device frame. The **C5** board **folds** into the device frame (§2a): `device_x = 75 − part_x` (a footprint interval `[x, x+w] → [75−(x+w), 75−x]`), `device_y = part_y` (Y is not mirrored). So "left / right" in a C5 row is that board's own edge — it lands on the **opposite** device edge after the fold. The §3 drawing mirror is **render-only** and never moves a physical edge.

## 8. Automated checks — all must read 0 before the render is used

The generator parses its own finished SVG and refuses to ship a render that fails any of these (never checked by eye):

1. **Overlap** — no rectangle / text collisions.
2. **Accessibility** — every **interface** (blue) on an **inner** face sits at a board edge (a buried connector / switch / button can't reach the case). Only the forward-firing round speaker is exempt (it needs no edge slot).
3. **Arrow-on-label** — no direction arrow lands on any label.
4. **Mezzanine gap** — the cross-section gap ≥ the tallest inner-face part.
5. **Mounting holes** — no part or antenna overlaps a corner M2.5 hole.
6. **Cross-board (clamshell fold)** — no two inner-face **side-edge** exits from the *two different boards* land on the same device side edge with overlapping height. The C5 board seats **X-mirrored** onto the main board (§2a), so a C5 **right**-edge exit maps to the device's **left** edge; both boards' side exits share the ~11 mm gap band and would fight for the same case slot (e.g. the 3.5 mm jack vs the IR). Bottom-edge exits are ~11 mm depth-staggered across the boards and may share X — the check still **lists** those cross-board pairs as info so the PCB gives each its own staggered cutout; only the forward-firing round speaker is exempt (the mic is a normal bottom-edge port).
7. **Mezzanine mate** — the two board-to-board halves land at the **same device X and Y** after the fold (§2), or they cannot connect.
