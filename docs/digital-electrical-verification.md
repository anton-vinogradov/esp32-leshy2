## Why 20 MHz

ILI9488 allows at most 25 MHz, but the standard ESP-IDF integer divider turns a 24-MHz request into 26.667 MHz. A 20-MHz request produces exactly 20 MHz: a 50-ns cycle, 25-ns WR phases and at least 10 ns of cycle/pulse margin.
