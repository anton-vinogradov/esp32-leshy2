Recipient: `ebyteiot@cdebyte.com`

Target product: Leshy2 handheld radio instrument

## Message

Subject: E01-ML01IPX samples, exact RF connector identity and production quote

Hello,

We are evaluating the exact `E01-ML01IPX` module for three simultaneous
full-function nRF24 radio paths in a new handheld product. Please quote four
engineering samples and later quantities of 100 and 500 units.

Your current official-store page publishes USD 2.02, but it also shows both an
out-of-stock/pre-order control and general in-stock shipping text. Please
confirm whether four exact `E01-ML01IPX` units can ship now, their lead time and
the actual sample price.

Before ordering, please answer the following:

1. What is the manufacturer and exact series/MPN of the miniature RF receptacle
   fitted to the current `E01-ML01IPX` production lot? The page uses both
   `IPX/IPEX` and `U.FL`; those family names are not sufficient for mechanical
   acceptance.
2. Please identify an exact mating cable assembly from that receptacle to a
   50-ohm SMA female bulkhead or PCB-end SMA jack. Include coax type, length,
   insertion loss and the mating-cycle rating.
3. Confirm that all four samples and the quoted production lot use genuine
   Nordic `nRF24L01P` and support 250 kbps, 1 Mbps and 2 Mbps, six receive data
   pipes, automatic acknowledgement/retransmit, ACK payload and dynamic payload.
4. Provide the current controlled module drawing, recommended land pattern,
   paste/reflow profile, maximum body/connector height and RF keepout.
5. State the current hardware revision, lot marking scheme, country of origin,
   RoHS/REACH status and whether any RF IC, crystal, matching-network or
   receptacle substitution is permitted without PCN.
6. Quote unit prices for 4 samples, 100 and 500 units, MOQ, packaging, sample and
   production lead times, lifecycle status and PCN/EOL notification terms.

Please keep the sample hardware revision identical to the quoted production
configuration, or list every difference explicitly.

Thank you.
