# H5.0.3-R1 no-order supplier inquiry

Status: prepared only; **not authorized to send**. This message requests
capability, lead-time and price information. It does not authorize a quote
project, reservation, sourcing order, component purchase, PCB work or
fabrication.

## Suggested recipient

JLCPCB PCBA/box-build support or another selected turnkey factory able to own
both PCBA and final product assembly. A fallback supplier must answer the same
questions so that responses remain comparable.

## Message

Subject: Leshy2 pre-order lead time and final-assembly capability — information only

Hello,

We are preparing a five-unit Leshy2 engineering prototype but are not placing
an order yet. Please answer the following without creating a reservation,
sourcing order or paid quote project.

1. For exact `G-NiceRF SA818S-V`, JLC part `C51897911`, confirm current sample
   lead time, MOQ, any pre-order/service charge and whether one unit can be
   installed by Standard PCBA. Please do not substitute another module.
2. Confirm whether the same job may place exact in-stock `G-NiceRF SA818S-U`,
   JLC part `C3001549`, beside the VHF variant while preserving their common
   manufacturer Rev 1.8 18-land footprint and separate RF paths.
3. State whether your final-assembly service accepts each `J4-F` operation:
   mate and test `HMX035CTFT-001` display/flex; install, strain-route and
   continuity-test five `TE Connectivity 2118651-2` microcoax jumpers; install
   `Davies 1227-J`; assemble the two-board sandwich/enclosure; and execute a
   supplied whole-device functional-test procedure.
4. For every accepted `J4-F` operation, provide setup/NRE, per-unit price at
   quantities 5 and 10, required customer fixtures/files, and reject/rework
   terms. List unsupported operations separately.
5. State whether your kitting/packing service accepts each `J4-P` operation:
   compatibility-test and pack `M5Stack U214 Cap LoRa-1262` separately; pack
   ten labelled external antennas as a kit; and pack two exact protected
   `XTAR 18650 4000 mAh 10 A` cells separately for user installation.
6. For the protected cells, state whether you can procure and ship them in the
   same parcel, which destination restrictions apply, and which compliance
   documents or separate battery shipment are required.
7. Confirm that all externally supplied exact MPNs remain identity-controlled
   through incoming inspection and that no silent component substitution is
   performed.

Please return a line-by-line yes/no answer, lead times and indicative prices.
This inquiry is for design-gate evidence only and is not an order request.

Thank you.

## Response acceptance

A usable response identifies the legal supplier entity and date, answers every
line explicitly, ties `SA818S-V` to `C51897911`, itemizes `J4-F` and `J4-P`
costs, and lists exclusions. Silence or a generic “box build supported” claim
does not close the gate.
