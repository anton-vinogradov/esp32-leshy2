## Response acceptance

The [official JLCPCB API-access article](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/jlcpcb-online-api-available-now)
states that applications are reviewed using previous JLCPCB orders, company
situation and business situation. That documents the decision class, not the
exact reason for this rejection.

A JLCPCB customer-support representative replied that the account is relatively
new and has no order history, so JLCPCB cannot presently verify a clear and
ongoing business need. The suggested routes are to build order history and
reapply, or provide a more detailed business case, integration plan or unique
requirement for possible exception review.

The responder explicitly stated that they are not a member of the API review
team. No exact order count/value threshold, review-team confirmation or
actionable application-field correction was provided. Parts therefore remains
`Rejected`; no new application was submitted, API calls remain unusable, and
the live manual catalogue/BOM route remains authoritative.
